ABOUT CALINO

A ceramic manufacturing partner built around your requirements.

Calino is a Romanian manufacturer of ceramic sanitary products, active since 2016. We combine European manufacturing with competitive costs, producing toilets, bidets, sinks and other ceramic sanitary ware for clients who outsource part of their production to us.
